Questions before you book

What actually happens on the visit?

For a repair, we diagnose the issue and explain the fix and cost before work begins.

For a replacement quote, we inspect your home and system, then show you what fits and what it costs.

You decide what to do next.

Do you do repairs?

Yes. Choose Repair in the form above and we will call to discuss a visit. You can also call or text (786) 822-6861 directly. The $99 diagnostic is credited toward your repair.

If your system is nearing the end of its life, we’ll show you replacement options too, instead of an expensive repair that won’t hold.

Is the visit free?

New unit quotes are free. We come out, inspect, and give you options. No trip fee, no obligation.

Repair diagnostics are $99, credited toward your repair. Use the form above or call or text (786) 822-6861.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. New systems include our 1-Year Labor Warranty and our System Workmanship Warranty, which covers our installation work for as long as you own your home. Repairs include a 90-Day Repair Warranty on the repair we perform.

See warranty details →

Do you offer financing?

Monthly payments are available on qualifying installs. We show numbers at the house, not buried in fine print.

What brands do you work on?

All the major brands. When a new system is the right call, we size it for your home and Miami humidity.

What areas do you serve?

South Florida, including Miami-Dade and nearby communities. Share your city when we call and we will confirm.

How fast can you install?

It depends on equipment and the job. After the visit you will know a real timeline.
