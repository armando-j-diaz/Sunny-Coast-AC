What we see in Pembroke Pines homes

A lot of Pembroke Pines was built in the 1990s and early 2000s. Many of those homes are on an older AC that’s starting to wear out.

Bigger homes and two-story homes can also have rooms that are hard to keep cool.

  • Systems reaching their limit. Older units lose cooling power and use more electricity.
  • Upstairs rooms running hot. We check airflow and system size, not just the unit.
  • Dirty coils. Dust and mold on the coils make your AC work harder.
