What salt air does to your AC

Fort Lauderdale homes near the water deal with salt in the air every day. Salt eats away at the metal fins on your outdoor unit over time.

When those fins wear out, your AC has to work harder to cool your home. That means higher bills and shorter life.

  • Rusty or flaky outdoor coil. A sign salt is doing damage. Catching it early can save the unit.
  • Rinse it with fresh water. A gentle rinse of the outdoor unit every few months helps wash salt off. No pressure washers.
  • Ask about coated coils. When it’s time for a new system, some coils are made to hold up better near the beach.
