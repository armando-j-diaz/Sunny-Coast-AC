What we see in South Miami homes

South Miami has many older homes with mature trees. Older homes often have older ductwork and tight AC closets.

Our team works in South Miami and all around it, in Coral Gables, Pinecrest, and Kendall. The problems we see most nearby are drain line clogs, refrigerant leaks, and worn capacitors.

  • Drain line clogs. Humidity grows slime in the line. It’s a top cause of water leaks.
  • Refrigerant leaks. Low refrigerant means a leak, not a refill. We find it first.
  • Older systems. If your AC is 12 to 15 years old or more, we’ll show you repair and new system options side by side.
