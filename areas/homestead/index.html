What we see in Homestead homes

Homestead gets some of the hottest, most humid days in South Florida. Dust from fields and new construction also clogs filters and coils faster.

We’re based here, so we know these homes and the problems they run into.

  • Dirty filters and coils. Extra dust means filters need changing more often.
  • Long run times. In our heat, ACs run almost all day. Worn parts show up sooner.
  • Drain line clogs. Humidity grows slime in the drain line. It’s a top cause of leaks.
