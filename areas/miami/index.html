What we see in Miami homes

Miami has every kind of home: high-rise condos, older houses, and newer townhomes. Each one has its own AC setup, and we work on all of them.

In condos, the indoor unit often sits in a small closet. A clogged drain line there can leak into the unit below, so we check it on every visit.

  • Drain line clogs. Our humidity grows slime in the line. It’s one of the most common causes of water leaks.
  • Tight closets. Air handlers squeezed into small spaces need clean filters and good airflow to keep up.
  • Year-round use. Miami ACs barely get a break, so small problems add up faster.
