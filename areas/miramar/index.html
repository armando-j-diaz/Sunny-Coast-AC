What we see in Miramar homes

Miramar has a mix of older neighborhoods in the east and newer homes in the west. Older homes often need repairs. Newer homes need good care to last.

Either way, most problems start small: a dirty filter, a clogged drain, or a worn part.

  • Clogged drain lines. A common cause of water on the floor. We clear it and check the safety switch.
  • Worn electrical parts. Capacitors and contactors wear out in our heat. They’re a common, affordable fix.
  • Thermostat problems. Sometimes the AC is fine and the thermostat is the issue.
