What we see in Palmetto Bay homes

Palmetto Bay has lots of family homes with big trees and humid air. That humidity grows slime in AC drain lines.

On many of our recent visits here, our check found no broken part at all. When something was wrong, it was most often the drain line, a refrigerant leak, or the thermostat.

  • Drain line clogs. The most common fix here. A clog can leak water or trip the safety switch and shut your AC off.
  • Thermostat problems. Sometimes the AC is fine and the thermostat is the problem. We check it first.
  • Refrigerant leaks. Low refrigerant means a leak. We find it before adding more.
