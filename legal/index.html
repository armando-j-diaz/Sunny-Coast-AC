1. Who we are

Sunny Coast AC is the retail brand of Cool Now South Florida LLC (“we,” “us”). Website: sunnycoastac.com. FL License: CAC1825130.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or download a guide, we may collect:

  • Name and contact info (email, phone)
  • Message content you send us (texts, emails, form notes)
  • Basic technical data (browser type, device, pages visited) via standard hosting logs

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Why we collect it

  • Respond to service requests and schedule work
  • Deliver guides by email and service-related messages you asked for
  • Improve quality and scheduling (including recordings / AI-assisted handling; see below)

4. Call & text recording / AI-assisted systems

Florida is a two-party consent state. Calls and texts to Sunny Coast AC may be recorded and/or handled by automated or AI-assisted systems for quality and scheduling. By calling or texting us, you consent to this.

Our phone line should also announce recording where required (operations note).

5. SMS terms (TCPA)

Service messages only. If you check the optional texting box on our quote request forms, you agree to receive service-related texts from Sunny Coast AC — appointment scheduling, technician arrival updates, and quotes. Up to 6 messages per month. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

The free AC guide at sunnycoastac.com/guide/ is delivered by email only. Checking the texting box is not required to get service, and it is never pre-checked. If you give us your phone number when you request a quote, a member of our team may call or text you personally about that request — that is how we schedule your visit.

For help, call (786) 822-6861 or email admin@sunnycoastac.com. See our Privacy Policy & SMS Terms.

Carriers are not liable for any delayed or undelivered messages.

6. How we share information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories below exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We use service providers (scheduling, messaging, hosting, automation tools) only as needed to run the business and deliver the service you asked for. We do not sell lists. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention & security

We keep contact and job records as long as needed for service, warranty, and legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards; no method of transmission is 100% secure.

8. Your choices

  • Do not submit info you do not want us to store
