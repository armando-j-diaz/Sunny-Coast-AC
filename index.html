How it works

Three steps, whether you need a repair or a new system.

  1. Book a visit

    Call, text, or request a visit online. New-unit quotes are free. Repair visits are $99, credited to your repair.

  2. We check the whole system

    We look at your AC, your home, and how South Florida humidity affects them. Repairs get our 9-point system check, so we fix the real problem.

  3. You get clear options and a price

    Straightforward recommendations and a flat price in writing before any work starts. You decide how to move forward.

Quick answers

Do you charge for the visit?

Replacement quotes are free — no trip fee, no obligation. We inspect your system, then walk you through clear options.

Diagnostic and repair visits are $99. That $99 is credited toward your repair if you go ahead with it.

Do you do repairs?

Yes. Choose Repair in the form below and we will call to discuss a visit. You can also call or text (786) 822-6861 directly. The $99 diagnostic is credited toward your repair.

If your system is at the end of its life, we'll tell you that instead of selling you a repair that won't hold.

What happens on a repair visit?

We start with what you are noticing and check the basics. Then we run our 9-point system check, in the same order every time, so nothing gets skipped or guessed.

We show you what we found and give you a flat price in writing before any work starts. The $99 visit is credited to your repair. See how a repair visit works →

Do you offer financing?

Monthly payments are available on qualifying installs. We review real numbers with you at the house.

What brands do you install?

We install major brands sized for your home and Miami humidity, not whatever is cheapest on the truck.

What areas do you serve?

South Florida, including Miami-Dade and nearby communities. Share your city when we call and we will confirm coverage.

How fast can you install?

Timing depends on equipment and the job. After the visit, you will have a clear timeline.
