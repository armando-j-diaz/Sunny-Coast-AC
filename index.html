How it works

Three steps, whether you need a repair or a new system.

  1. Book a visit

    Call, text, or book online. If your AC is broken or acting up, it’s a free repair estimate. If you already know you want a new system, the estimate is free too.

  2. We check the whole system

    We look at your AC, your home, and how South Florida humidity affects them. Repairs get our 18-point system check, so nothing gets missed. See what we check.

  3. We help you choose

    We show you what we found and explain your options in plain words. You get a flat price in writing before any work starts, and you decide what to do. Every job comes with our written warranty.

Quick answers

Do you charge for the visit?

No. It’s a free repair estimate. We run our 18-point check and give you a flat price in writing. You only pay for the work you approve. If you decide not to repair, you owe nothing for the visit.

If you already know you want a new system, the estimate is free, with no obligation. Either way, we check the whole system and walk you through clear options.

Do you do repairs?

Yes. Choose Repair in our booking form and we will call to discuss a visit. You can also call or text (786) 822-6861 directly. It’s a free repair estimate, and you only pay for the work you approve.

If your system is nearing the end of its life, we’ll show you replacement options too, instead of an expensive repair that won’t hold.

What happens on a repair visit?

We start with what you are noticing and check the basics. Then we run our 18-point system check, in the same order every time, so nothing gets skipped or guessed.

We show you what we found and give you a flat price in writing before any work starts. You only pay for the work you approve. See how a repair visit works →
