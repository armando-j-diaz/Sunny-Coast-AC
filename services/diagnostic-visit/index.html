What our 18-point check covers

Controls

  • Thermostat settings and wiring

Airflow

  • Air filter
  • Airflow at the vents
  • Blower wheel and motor
  • Ductwork you can see

Indoor unit

  • Indoor coil (dirt and ice)
  • Drain line, pan, and safety switch

Electrical

  • Breaker and outdoor shut-off
  • Capacitors
  • Contactor and wiring

Outdoor unit

  • Outdoor coil
  • Fan motor and blade
  • Compressor (sound, start, power draw)

Refrigerant

  • Pressures, with digital gauges
  • Charge (is it the right amount?)
  • Temperature drop (air in vs. air out)
  • Leak check

Overall

  • Unit age and condition

Quick answers

Is the $99 extra on top of my repair?

No. If you go ahead with the repair, the $99 is credited to it.

What if I don’t want the repair?

That’s your call. The $99 covers the visit and the diagnosis. You keep the findings.

Why isn’t the visit free?

A real diagnosis takes time, tools, and a trained tech. “Free” visits often turn into a sales pitch. Ours is a real check, and the $99 goes toward your repair.

I already want a new system. Do I pay $99?

No. If you already know you want a new system, the quote visit is free.

Do you check everything, even if I know the problem?

Yes. We start with what you’re noticing, then check the whole system. That way the real cause gets fixed, not just the symptom.
