Below are the most common AC problems we see, and what they usually mean.

Signs your AC needs help

  • Blowing warm air. Often a dirty filter, a bad part outside, or low refrigerant.
  • Water inside the house. Usually a clogged drain line. Turn the AC off and call us before it damages your floor or ceiling.
  • New or loud noises. Buzzing, grinding, or banging means a part is wearing out.
  • Ice on the copper lines. Turn the AC off and let it melt. Running it frozen can hurt the compressor.
  • Runs all day but never cools. The system is working too hard. Something is keeping it from cooling right.
  • Power bill jumped. An AC that’s struggling uses more power.

Not sure? Call us and tell us what you’re noticing. We’ll help you figure out the next step.
