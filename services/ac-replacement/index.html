Signs it may be time

  • It’s 12 to 15 years old or more. South Florida heat wears ACs out faster than in most places.
  • Repairs keep adding up. If you’re paying for a big repair every summer, a new system may cost less over time.
  • It uses R-22. Older ACs use a refrigerant that isn’t made anymore. It’s very expensive to refill.
  • Some rooms never cool. The system may be the wrong size, or just worn out.
  • Bills keep climbing. Newer systems use a lot less power for the same cooling.

Repair or replace? A quick check

There’s no perfect rule. These are the questions we go over with you.

A repair usually makes sense if:

  • It’s under 10 years old.
  • This is its first big problem.
  • The repair costs well under half the price of a new system.
  • Your power bills have stayed about the same.

A new system usually makes sense if:

  • It’s 12 to 15 years old or more. Miami heat and salt air wear ACs out sooner.
  • You’ve paid for big repairs more than once lately.
  • It uses R-22. That old refrigerant isn’t made anymore.
  • The repair costs half the price of a new system or more.
  • Bills keep going up, or some rooms never cool.

Not sure? At a repair visit, if your AC is old and the repair is big, we show you both choices side by side, with prices in writing.

How getting a new system works

  1. Free estimate

    We look at your home, your current system, and your ducts. We ask how your home feels and what you’d like to improve.

  2. Clear options in writing

    You get a few options with prices. We explain each one in plain words. No pressure.

  3. We pull the permit

    Every new system install gets a permit from your city or county. We file it for you.

  4. Install day

    We take out the old system, put in the new one, and clean up after ourselves.

  5. Test and walk-through

    We test everything, show you how it works, and go over your warranty.

  6. City inspection

    Because there’s a permit, an inspector from your city or county checks the install. We set up the time with you.

What goes into the price

  • Size. AC size is measured in tons. Most homes need between 2 and 5 tons. Bigger costs more.
  • Efficiency (SEER2). A higher SEER2 number costs more up front and less every month on your power bill.
  • Type of system. A split system (one part inside, one outside), a packaged unit on the roof or ground, or ductless.
  • Where it goes. A closet is the simplest. Attics, roofs, and tight spaces take more work.
  • What’s around it. Sometimes the stand or pad, the drain line, the outdoor power switch, or the duct connections need updating too.
  • The permit. Every install needs one, so it’s part of the price.
  • Rebates. Some high-efficiency systems qualify for a rebate from your power company.

Your free estimate gives you exact prices in writing, for each option.

One-stage, two-stage, or variable speed?

  • One-stage. It’s either off or running full blast. Lowest price. Fine for many homes.
  • Two-stage. It runs on low most of the day and goes to high on the hottest afternoons. Longer, quieter runs pull more water out of the air.
  • Variable speed. It speeds up and slows down in small steps. Most even temperature, best humidity control, lowest power bills, highest price.

In Miami, humidity matters as much as temperature. A system that runs longer at low speed dries the air better. We’ll tell you if it’s worth it for your home.

Before your estimate

  • How old your system is. The sticker on the unit often shows the year it was made.
  • Which rooms run hot. Or any rooms that feel damp or sticky.
  • Your questions. Write them down. We’ll answer every one.

FPL rebate on qualifying new systems

We are an approved FPL Participating Independent Contractor. FPL offers a $200 instant rebate on qualifying new AC systems. It shows up as a credit on your invoice.

  • You have an FPL home electric account
  • It is a full new system, inside and outside units
  • The system meets FPL’s efficiency rules
  • Mini-splits and window units do not qualify

We check if your new system qualifies before you buy, so there are no surprises.

Quick answers

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, look at your home, and give you options in writing. No obligation.

What if I just need a repair?

Then it’s a free repair estimate, and we run our 18-point check. You only pay for the repair you approve.

If the repair doesn’t make sense, we’ll show you new system options too.

How do you pick the right size?

We look at your home, not just the old unit. A system that’s too big or too small costs more to run and wears out faster.

How long does an install take?

Most new system installs are done in one day. If yours needs more time, we tell you up front.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, financing is available on qualifying installs. Ask us about it during your quote visit.

What warranty do I get?

Every new install comes with our 1-Year Labor Warranty plus our System Workmanship Warranty, on top of the manufacturer’s warranty.

Do you pull a permit?

Yes. Every new system install gets a permit. An inspector from your city or county checks the work after we finish.

What refrigerant do new systems use?

New systems are moving to newer refrigerants like R-32 and R-454B. We’ll tell you which one each option uses and what it means for future repairs.

How long does a new AC last in Miami?

Often 10 to 15 years. Salt air near the coast and skipped maintenance can shorten it. A tune-up once or twice a year helps.
