What happens on a repair visit

No guessing. We check the whole system in order, show you what we found, and price it before we touch anything.

  1. We start with what you are noticing

    Warm air, water on the floor, a strange noise, a high bill. Tell us what is going on.

  2. Our 9-point system check

    We test the system in the same order every time, so nothing gets skipped and nothing gets guessed.

    We do not jump to “it needs Freon.” Most cooling problems start with airflow, dirty coils or electrical parts, so we check those first.

    • Thermostat and controls
    • Airflow and filter
    • Frozen coil check
    • Electrical parts
    • Temperature drop
    • Refrigerant readings
    • Leak check
    • Compressor
    • Ductwork

  3. We show you what we found

    You see the photos and readings. If a repair makes sense, you get a flat price in writing before any work starts. Your $99 visit is credited to the repair.

    If your system is nearing the end of its life, we’ll show you replacement options too, instead of an expensive repair that won’t hold.

  4. We fix it, test it and walk you through it

    After the repair we test the system again to make sure it is cooling the way it should. Then we show you what we did. Your repair is covered by our 90-Day Repair Warranty.
