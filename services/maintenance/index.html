AC Maintenance · Miami-Dade & Broward

A clean AC lasts longer and costs less to run.

Miami heat and humidity are hard on an AC. A tune-up catches small problems before they turn into big, expensive ones.

What a tune-up does

  • Clears the drain line. One of the most common causes of water leaks in South Florida homes.
  • Cleans what’s dirty. Dirty coils and filters make your AC work harder and cost more to run.
  • Checks the electrical parts. Worn parts get caught before they fail on the hottest day of the year.
  • Checks the refrigerant. We make sure the system has the right amount and isn’t leaking.
  • Tells you where you stand. You get an honest look at your AC’s health and age.

Quick answers

How often should my AC get a tune-up?

At least once a year. In South Florida, where the AC runs almost all year, twice a year is even better.

Why does my drain line keep clogging?

Our humidity helps slime and algae grow in the drain line. A tune-up clears it. Pouring a cup of white vinegar in the drain line once a month helps too.

How much is a tune-up?

Call us for current tune-up pricing. If we find something broken, we show you first and give you a flat price before any repair.

What can I do myself?

Change your filter every month. Keep 2 feet clear around the outdoor unit. Our free South Florida AC guide walks you through it.
