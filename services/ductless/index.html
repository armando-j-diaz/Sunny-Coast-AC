Good for

  • Garages and workshops. Cool a space your main AC doesn’t reach.
  • Add-ons and converted rooms. No need to run new ducts.
  • That one hot bedroom. Give it its own AC and its own temperature.
  • Older homes. Some older South Florida homes don’t have ducts at all.

How it works

  1. Outside

    A small outdoor unit sits on the ground or on a wall bracket.

  2. Inside

    A slim unit goes high on the wall. It blows cool air right into the room.

  3. In between

    A small line set runs through a hole about 3 inches wide. No ductwork needed.

Quick answers

What is a ductless mini-split?

It’s an AC with a small outdoor unit and a slim indoor unit on the wall. A small line connects them. No ducts needed.

Can one outdoor unit cool more than one room?

Yes. Multi-zone systems can run several indoor units, and each room can have its own temperature.

Do you repair mini-splits too?

Yes. We repair mini-splits that aren’t cooling, are leaking, show error codes, or have a dirty blower. It’s a free repair estimate, and you only pay for the repair you approve.

How do I keep it working well?

Rinse the filters every month. Get a deep cleaning once a year. Dirty mini-splits can grow mold inside.

How much does one cost?

It depends on how many rooms and the size. The estimate for a new system is free, and you get prices in writing.
